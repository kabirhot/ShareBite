Privacy Policy

ShareBite is a student project built at Thadomal Shahani Engineering College, Mumbai. This page explains what information the website collects and why.

What we collect

Account: your name, email address, phone number and a password (stored only as a hash, never as plain text).

Forms: the details you type into the donor, receiver, volunteer and contact forms, such as food details, pickup or delivery address, date and time.

Why we collect it

Only to connect food donors, receivers and volunteers. Donations and food requests, including the contact number and address you enter, are shown to other logged-in users so that food can be picked up and delivered.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics scripts. The site sets no cookies. Your login is kept in your browser tab and is removed when you log out or close the tab.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time through the contact page. This policy is written with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) in mind.
